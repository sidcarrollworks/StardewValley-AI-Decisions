How to read these numbers

Repeat risk: three or fewer fitting lines. The selector avoids a speaker's last three lines for an act, so a small pool makes repetition more likely.

No voice-specific lines: no fitting line is tagged for that archetype. Generic lines can still be available; this is a voice gap, not necessarily silence.

The grid pairs an act with a voice. Its small squares show relationship and mood combinations; filters narrow the grid. These summary metrics always cover the full catalog.
