Cue examples and limits

Cues connect a memory to a scene. Use person:Name, place:exact-place-name or activity:PlayedGame. A memory alone is recallable; a present attitude supplies a small starting feeling. Warmth and wariness stay separate. Finished pasts remain recall-only here.

These are explicit experimental starting conditions, not canon findings. Editing a sourced account creates an experimental version in your draft. The shipped pack stays untouched. Histories shape Lives choices; the separate inspiration prompt still uses the character profile.
