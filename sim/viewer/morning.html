How to follow a thought

Try one person, one thought

  1. Follow someone. Choose a person on the map or use a Follow button in Happening now.
  2. Move to the next moment. Use Next moment to skip quiet stretches while following that person.
  3. Open their inner life. Use Follow this thought to trace its memory, choice, action and recorded outcome. Some intentions may remain unresolved.
  4. Inspect the evidence. Expand What informed this choice? for candidate lines, weights and exact model receipts. The clock hides later events; Stories offers a separate whole-run analysis switch.

A dream is an imagined possibility

When a recording includes waking dreams, look for Dream on waking. Its images and ideas belong to that person’s private imagination.

They do not establish that an encounter happened or that someone witnessed it. The remembered source and the later recorded action remain separate.
