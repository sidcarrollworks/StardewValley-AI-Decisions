How to read a card
Status pill
What the villager is doing now, in words. would wave it acts; would glare it acts on a hurt; close call the model decides; waved · waiting on you it tried to get your attention and waits for you to answer (talk to it); holding back it has a reason but can't act, and says why; calm nothing on its mind.
Feeling
The motive it would act on and how strong it is, 0 to 1. Green is friendly, red hostile. Below it, the other feelings it has.
What it would dare
Every act, cheapest first. ✓ it would do it; ? a close call for the model; struck through: too shy for it; faded: not possible now (you're away, a letter is already out, or the feeling is too weak for it). The outlined one is its pick.
Tiles
Regard is its lasting feeling toward you (-1 to +1; -0.75 is a grudge). Mood is today's outlook. Tries counts today's attempts. Saw you is when it last saw you.
Tabs
Why: its feelings and where they come from, its mood and its daring sum. About you: where it saw you, where it would look, tonight's news. Then the old urge ladder, its temperament and its diary.
