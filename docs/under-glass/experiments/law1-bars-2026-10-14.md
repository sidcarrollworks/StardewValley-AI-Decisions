# Law 1 and the bars: general, particular or Spinoza — 2026-10-14

Sid asked how the three readings of the amended law 1 play out ([needs bars spec](../specs/needs-bars-spec.md),
"Law 1, amended"). This runs them. Branch `claude/bars-experiment`: the spec's step 1 (company and fun
kept and recorded, `--catalog bars`) plus an experimental `BarMode`:

| Mode | What a bar does |
|---|---|
| 0, watch | nothing: kept and recorded only (byte-identical to bars off: seed 7's 28-day hash `2aab98deec17dd78` either way) |
| 1, general (the amendment as written) | leans the free-time pick toward any gathering and the Saloon (company) and any pastime (fun), by min(cap, 1 + gain x pressure); grows Fond's and Missing's wish; eases yes to an invitation |
| 2, particular (the recommendation) | as 1, but a place leans with loneliness by how many of the person's own good chats of the last 14 days were there, and with boredom by how many of their own outings |
| 3, Spinoza | a bar is a sadness of the body: it lowers the power of acting (`Conditions`) and nothing else reads it |

Company: waking minutes since a good chat (anyone not disliked, or a game or drink together), lonely past
twice one's own median longest daily stretch (six hours at least). Fun: minutes since an outing (an hour at
the Beach, the Saloon from 17:00 or the Market), bored past 2.5 times one's usual gap (two days at least),
only for someone with a pastime. Children never. The first week settles and is left out of every figure.

Six seeds of 28 days each, the shipped town with `--feel on --desire on --catalog
returns,company,welcome,repair,sides,invitations,bars`, at two strengths: **default** (the spec's numbers:
gain 1, cap 2, power weight 0.3) and **amplified** (lonely past 1.25 times one's usual, three hours at
least; bored past 1.25 times, a day at least; gain 3, cap 4; power weight 0.5). Recordings in
`out/bars-2026-10-14/` (ignored); the analysis script is in the PR description.

## At the spec's strength, nothing visibly changes

Town-wide, 11 lonely and 7 bored hours a week across 26 people: the bars, measured against each person's
own life, rarely press, and all four modes give the same town to the first decimal (chats 25.9 a
person-day, 5.7 hours out, 88.5 drinks a week, 67 kind and 7.5 hostile acts a week). People chat often
(about 26 times a day); a stretch twice one's usual without one is rare. This is the spec's "too subtle to
see" risk, and its tuning rule (For Sid 4) would raise the strength after step 1.

- **Spinoza: loneliness becomes sadness, with no way out.** Low-power days rise ninefold, in every seed,
  and boredom is not relieved (worse in three seeds, the same in three). In this sim nothing reads power
  when choosing where to go or whom to seek, so a sadder person is not drawn to company; they are only
  sadder (and, through power, less daring, with the drinker tilt and contagion reaching others).
- **General and particular both relieve boredom, by about a fifth,** in every seed, and loneliness a
  little (a few percent, inside the seeds' spread), at about one more drink bought a week (more evenings at
  the Saloon). Between them the town-wide differences are inside the noise: hostile acts move between 3
  and 12 a week by seed whatever the mode.
- **The difference is in who goes where.** Particular relieves people through their own habits; general
  sends everyone toward the same gatherings and the Saloon. Per person, six seeds together (lonely hours,
  bored hours):

- **Why the effect is small even amplified.** A bar leans only the free-time pick, made every one to
  three hours outside work, sleep and errands; most of a day is routine. Lonely days already have fewer
  chats (about 19 against 27) because they are days spent alone, at work or at home, where no pick is made.

## What it means for law 1

- **Pure Spinoza, read as "sadness only", is not Spinoza's whole account.** Law 4 (III P12-P13, P28) has
  desire aim at what was joyful before. The sim has that for particular people (Fond, Missing) but not for
  places and pastimes, so a sadness with nowhere to go is what this mode shows: suffering without seeking.
- **The particular lean is the missing half:** desire toward one's own remembered joys. Spinoza done in
  full here would be the particular lean plus, optionally, a small felt sadness while a bar presses (well
  under this run's 0.5, since power reaches daring, tone and speech).
- **General works about as well in numbers** but makes everyone's loneliness look the same (the gathering,
  the Saloon), which is the "list of needs" the old sentence ruled out, and it reads nobody's history.

## Limits

28 days, of which 21 counted; six seeds; once one pick changes, a run diverges, so per-person figures carry
chance as well as the mode. The amplified numbers are for contrast, not proposals. Drinks are counted from
the Saloon's daily charge, not from acts. Nothing here touches Penny's concern, core memories or hunger.
